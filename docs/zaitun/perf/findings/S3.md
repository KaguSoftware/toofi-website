## How it was measured

- **Bytes per mesh, attribute and texture**: `glb-cost.mjs` reads each GLB's JSON and binary chunk directly and counts what is stored (meshopt streams as stored, not decoded) and its brotli size. Sizes are decimal MB, as in BASELINE.md. Wire sizes are gzip -9 (what `next start` sends today) and brotli -11 (what a host could send, S6).
- **The look of a change**: each candidate is a byte-exact variant of the shipped file. `strip-glb.mjs` removes texture slots and attributes and copies every other buffer view byte for byte (meshopt streams are never decoded; its octahedral normal filter would drift on a round trip). `ktx2-mip.mjs` drops a texture's top mip level, which is exactly what the GPU already shows one level down, so the test measures size alone, with no encoder noise. Both outputs pass `ktx validate` and decode in the browser. The variant goes into a scratch worktree (`zaitun/S3`, experiment builds only, never merged) and through the P0 look gate (`frames.mjs reference`: 11 cameras × 2 tiers, 960 px wide, virtual time). One change per run.
- **Grain.** The post chain's film grain is per-pixel noise whose pattern moves when anything changes three's random stream (one texture fewer is enough). A raw comparison then shows speckle over the sky and water of every frame (0.3–1.1/255) that is not a change to the scene. `compare.mjs --block 4` compares 4 × 4-pixel averages, which cancels grain and keeps real changes; both numbers are given. Variants stacked on V1 keep V1's texture count, hence its grain, so `--against` V1 compares them exactly.
- **Removable geometry**: `simplify-probe.mjs` (meshopt `simplifyWithAttributes`, absolute error in metres, UV, lightmap UV, colour and normal seams kept).
- Machine, browser and build as in BASELINE.md (`main` at 09684f0; no product change since the reference build 85b07fa).

## Findings

### S3-1: the AO maps and `TEXCOORD_1` do nothing on Zaitun (largest single saving)

**Evidence.** `bakedLight.ts:193` (`bakeMaterial`) sets `aoMapIntensity = 0` on every lit copy, and `setBakedStrength` (`bakedLight.ts:115`, which would fade AO back in) is called only from tests, so the strength stays 1 and AO stays 0. Every `-atlas` material is baked (opaque standard, has `uv2`). The only materials that keep AO are the two court lattices (`amenity-tennis-lattice`, `amenity-padel-lattice`), which `UNBAKED_MATERIAL` leaves on real-time light. The maps are still downloaded and still sampled by the shader (`USE_AOMAP` is defined whenever the map exists).

**Look test.**

| Variant | High | Low |
|---|---|---|
| All AO and `TEXCOORD_1` removed | raw 0.33–1.14/255 (all pass), grain-free ≤ 0.48 except stop-4 0.41 with **4.6% moved**, walk 1.9% moved: the court lattices turn lighter | same pattern |
| **V1: removed except on `-lattice` materials** | raw 0.33–1.14 (all pass); **grain-free ≤ 0.31, 0.0% of pixels moved by more than 4/255 on every camera** | grain-free ≤ 0.34, ≤ 0.1% moved |

The remaining grain-free residue sits in the sky band of every frame (grain leaking through the averaging), not on any surface.

**Bytes (V1).**

| File | Today | V1 | Saved |
|---|---|---|---|
| compound-zaitun-high.glb | 10.26 MB file, **7.14 gzip**, 6.79 brotli | 7.09, **4.41 gzip**, 4.13 brotli | **−2.73 MB gzip (−38%)** |
| compound-zaitun-low.glb | 4.97 MB file, **3.10 gzip**, 2.88 brotli | 3.63, **2.04 gzip**, 1.88 brotli | **−1.06 MB gzip (−34%)** |

**Measured** (trace.mjs, V1 in the worktree, 3 cold + 3 warm loads per profile, against BASELINE.md):

| Profile | Live scene (cold, median) | Bytes before live | Blocking to live | First content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| phone (low tier) | 7.10 → **6.07 s** (−1.03 s) | 5.34 → **4.28 MB** | 684 → 676 ms (unchanged) | 532 → 528 ms |
| desktop (high tier) | 1.46 → **1.36 s** | 10.83 → **8.10 MB** | 317 → 288 ms | 388 → 364 ms |

That is about two fifths of the way from 7.1 s to the 5 s target on the phone, and a third of the way to both transfer targets (5 MB high, 3 MB low). The live-scene gain is smaller than the bytes alone suggest (the model download overlaps the 3D code and the libraries). The trim is also less GPU memory (15 textures fewer, 2.2 MB of ETC1S plus their mip chains, decoded) and one texture sample fewer per baked pixel.

**Fix.** In `scripts/blender/export-final.sh` (F2B's file; F2A owns `scripts/blender/**` until then): for the Zaitun plan, drop `occlusionTexture` and `TEXCOORD_1` from every material except `-lattice`, or bake the lattices' AO into their `COLOR_0` and drop all of it. `strip-glb.mjs --slot occlusion --attr TEXCOORD_1 --keep 'lattice$'` produces the tested file and can stand in until the export is changed. Keep AO on the Bustan Dijla and Shatt models (`compound-*.glb`): they have no baked light. If `setBakedStrength` is ever wired up (a fade from real-time to baked light), AO would be needed again during the fade; nothing uses it today. Note 6B's history: the owner chose to keep AO when it was thought to show; the measurement says it no longer does.

### S3-2: the normal map can go one mip lower (high tier)

The baked light replaces the diffuse light with the lightmap, which uses the geometric normal (`FRAGMENT_MAPS` in bakedLight.ts), so on baked surfaces the normal map only shapes specular highlights and reflections.

- **V2** (V1 + `atlas-normal` 1024² → 512²): against V1, **0.00/255 on 9 of 11 high cameras**, stop-2 0.03 (0.1% of pixels: a glint along a hedge top and a kerb edge in the near foreground), walk 0.02. Texture 0.71 → 0.20 MB, **−0.50 MB** on the wire (file 7.09 → 6.58 MB, 4.41 → 3.91 gzip).
- Alternative at the same size: UASTC with RDO from the 2048² master (`ktx create --encode uastc --uastc-rdo-l 1|2`): 0.70 → 0.57 / 0.49 MB (−19% / −30%). Not look-tested.
- **At 1440 px** (the widest the rig captures: the canvas is read at the viewport's CSS width, so the 2520 px device buffer itself is not compared), V1 against V2 on the four closest cameras: stop-1 0.000, stop-2 0.025 raw / 0.13% of pixels moved after block averaging (the same hedge glint), stop-3 and walk 0.00% moved. Invisible.

### S3-3: the base colour and ORM atlases can go one mip lower (high tier)

- **V5** (V1 + `atlas-base` and `atlas-orm` 2048² → 1024², which is what the low tier ships): against V1, 0.00 on 6 cameras, ≤ 0.07 elsewhere (orbit 0.40 and stop-4 0.47 are those cameras' sky noise; 0.2% moved). **−0.74 MB brotli** (4.13 → 3.39 MB brotli; file 7.09 → 6.35 MB).
- **At 1440 px**, V1 against V5 on stop-1, stop-2, stop-3 and walk: 0.00% of pixels moved after block averaging on all four (worst pixel 10/255, the paving joints nearest the camera on walk). Invisible at the rig's cameras; a visitor walking right up to a wall in the explorer is the case not covered.

### S3-4: the vegetation atlas at half size is a look decision

- **V3** (`vegetation-atlas-high` 2048² → 1024²): passes the gate against the references (1.23 worst), but against V1 **1–2.7% of pixels move** at most cameras (worst pixel 158): frond and leaf edges, the olive groves' texture and the shadows they throw. It reads as the same scene; it is not the same pixels. 1.23 → 0.67 MB (**−0.56 MB**, 1.07 → 0.51 MB gzip).
- For the owner with the side-by-side frames (`scripts/perf/results/S3/look-v3-vs-v1/`). If declined, a smaller step is a 1536² atlas re-encoded from the master.

### S3-5: the poster is fetched at Low priority

The poster is Home's Largest Contentful Paint element on mobile and desktop (Lighthouse `largest-contentful-paint-element`: `.xp__poster > img`). It uses `<Image … priority>` (`ZaitunExperience.tsx:310`); Next 16 deprecated `priority` (node_modules/next/dist/docs/…/image.md) and the request goes out at **Low** priority (trace: `priority: "Low"`), behind the fonts. Lighthouse's `lcp-discovery-insight` fails on exactly this ("fetchpriority=high should be applied"). Fix: `fetchPriority="high"` (Next 16's recommendation over `preload`). Lighthouse's own simulated LCP (4.3 s) is dominated by main-thread time (S1), so the score gain is small; the gain is on real 4G, where the poster competes with 280 KB of fonts and chunks in the first second.

### S3-6: stills downloaded on the live-story path

Every beat renders `<Image className="xp__still" …>` whatever the visitor gets (`ZaitunExperience.tsx:389`), because the server renders the stills version first. On the live path the first still(s) enter the lazy-load margin during hydration and download: `zaitun-entrance` (72 KB) on the phone, `zaitun-entrance` + `zaitun-boulevard` (162 + 68 KB) on desktop, all before the model request. Fix: skip the still images (or `display: none` them, which stops lazy loading) once `data-state="story"`; keep them for stills, reduced motion and no-JS.

### S3-7: AVIF for the photos

`next.config.ts` keeps the default `images.formats` (WebP only; confirmed: sharp's WebP at q75 matches the served bytes, 24 vs 25 KB and 157 vs 161 KB). AVIF at comparable quality is about 25–30% smaller: poster at 1200 w 24 → about 18 KB, entrance still 70 → about 50 KB. About 25 KB on the phone and 80 KB on desktop before the live scene. Lighthouse's `image-delivery-insight` already passes on mobile. Small; cheap to take with `formats: ['image/avif', 'image/webp']`, but AVIF encodes are slow on the first request of each size (cache them at the host).

### S3-8: the music file

4.47 MB MP3, 117 kbps, 5:06, streamed with `preload="none"` only once sound is turned on (`audio.ts:280`), so it costs nothing before the live scene. Per listener on mobile data: AAC-LC 96 kbps 3.74 MB (−16%, plays everywhere), Opus 64 kbps 2.73 MB (−39%; needs `canPlayType` with an AAC fallback for older Safari). Encoded from the WAV master in `out/music/`. A bed under ambience tolerates the lower rate, but the owner should listen first.

### S3-9: lightmap PNG masters in `public/`

12 PNG masters (11.6 MB) in `public/sawad/lightmaps/zaitun/` are tracked and deploy with the site; the app only ever fetches the KTX2 beside them. Move them out of `public/` (for example `scripts/blender/out/lightmaps/`) and keep `lightmaps.json`'s names working (`bakedPageUrl` already swaps `.png` for `.ktx2`). Deploy size and repository weight only; no visitor gain.

### S3-10: what the remaining geometry is (input for F2A and F2B)

After S3-1 the high compound's geometry is about 2.26 MB brotli: `TEXCOORD_2` 637 KB, `TEXCOORD_0` 546, `COLOR_0` 490, `POSITION` 490, `NORMAL` 90. The site mesh (streets, kerbs, sidewalks, walls, gates) is 32% of it.

- `COLOR_0` is per face corner (palette tint × dust, `build_compound.py:1272`). Together with the two UV sets it splits almost every vertex (site: 122,633 vertices for 69,444 triangles), which is why a simplifier finds nothing to remove at 1 cm (below). F2A's far set should simplify before the split or treat these seams as free on the far levels, or it will find little to cut.
- Distance levels are the geometry lever, not cleanup.

## Scripts

| Script | What it does |
|---|---|
| `glb-cost.mjs` | Bytes per mesh, attribute and texture of a GLB (stored, decoded, brotli), and which material uses each image in which slot |
| `strip-glb.mjs` | Byte-exact removal of texture slots and attributes (`--keep` spares materials by name) |
| `ktx2-mip.mjs` | Drops the top mip of a KTX2 file or of named images inside a GLB, without re-encoding |
| `compare.mjs` | Side-by-side frames with an amplified heat map for every camera of a reference run; `--block` cancels grain, `--against` compares two runs |
| `simplify-probe.mjs` | Triangles a simplifier could remove per mesh at a given error in metres |

Tests beside each (`*.test.ts`). The rig itself was not changed. One rig issue for P0's owner: `rig.mjs:73` (`machineInfo`) reads `node_modules/next/package.json` from the checkout even when `nodeModulesSource` fell back to the main checkout's `node_modules`, so a worktree without its own install builds and then crashes. Installing in the worktree (`pnpm install --frozen-lockfile`) works around it.
