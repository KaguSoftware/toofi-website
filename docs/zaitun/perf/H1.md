# H1: the story as video, the 3D on request

Status: awaiting approval. Branch `zaitun/H1`, uncommitted.

## What changed and why

Owner's decision of 2026-11-24, outside the plan's track table: Home no longer draws the story live. It plays it as video ("the reel") and loads the live 3D only when the visitor presses "Explore". The reel was recorded from the live scene by `scripts/render-story-reel.mjs`, with every tree at its finest level (capture only), so the explorer opens on the same world. The reason is cost: the live story needed the 3D code, the models and a GPU before the first moving picture; the reel needs a video decoder.

How it works is in `components/sawad/experience/README.md` ("What a visitor gets", "The story", "The reel"). In short: the opening (16 s) plays by itself over the poster, started by the early inline script before React arrives; the tour (24 s) is never played, the scroll seeks it on the live story's easing; Explore fetches the 3D chunks and models then, fades the canvas in on the shot the reel shows, and Back removes the canvas.

## Who gets what

| Visitor | Gets |
| --- | --- |
| Nearly everyone (any GPU, any memory) | The reel. No 3D code, models or WebGL context until Explore. |
| Reduced motion, Save-Data, 2g, no JS | The stills (reduced motion also gets "Explore in 3D"). No video request. |
| A link reported as 3g, under 4 GB, under 4 threads | The reel, and an Explore button naming the download. |
| No WebGL2 or a software renderer | The reel, no Explore button. |
| A reel that fails in every codec | The stills, reported as `StoryReel`. A codec that fails gives way to the next one, reported as a warning. |

## What was checked in the browser

Chrome with the GPU on, driven through `scripts/perf/browser.mjs`; desktop 1440x900 and phone 412x915 with touch; `/` and `/ar`; dev server for function, production server for bytes.

- On load: the poster, then the opening plays by itself (`.xp__reel[data-visible="true"]`); `currentSrc` ends in the wide file on desktop and the tall file on the phone shape; `currentTime` advances. No `.glb`, `.ktx2`, `basis_transcoder` or three.js request before Explore, no `<canvas>`. No errors and no hydration warnings.
- Controls: pause, sound, Explore, "Skip the intro"; no day/dusk. Pause held the time still, play resumed.
- Scroll: `data-shown` goes to `tour`. At each of the five stops, with the scroll placed where `scrollToPosition` reaches the beat's `at`, the tour's `currentTime` was exactly `tourSeekTime(at)` (0 frames off) on both shapes and both locales: 2.350, 6.017, 11.350, 17.350, 24.017 s. One caption at a time, showing that stop's title, over that stop's shot. Back at the top the tour sits on frame 0 (the gate).
- Explore: "Loading the 3D view" shows, the 3D requests start then, the canvas fades in on the shot the reel was showing (a short flight in, or the gate), the page scroll locks (`overflow: hidden` on the root), and the explorer controls show (Back, Orbit, Walk, zoom, reset, day/dusk, sound). A click on a home marker opens the unit panel. Back removes the canvas and the reel is there and moves with the scroll again. A second Explore shows the loading note again until the new first frame.
- Reduced motion: stills and an "Explore in 3D" button.
- Reel blocked (`**/sawad/reel/**` aborted): the stills and an "Explore in 3D" button; the failure is reported as `StoryReel`, nothing uncaught.
- Arabic: the same, with Arabic captions and controls.
- After the codecs went in (Chrome, `/`, both shapes): the AV1 files are the ones fetched, and all five stops are again 0 frames off. With the AV1 files aborted, or served as junk bytes, the HEVC files play instead; with AV1 and HEVC aborted, H.264. Nothing uncaught. Not checked: Safari, Firefox, a real phone.

Fixed on the way (two defects, with tests):

1. Reduced motion, Save-Data and 2g visitors still fetched the opening video: `StoryReel` gave the video its file as soon as it mounted, which is before React has decided the visitor gets the stills. It now reads the same decision as the early script (`playsReel`, without the GPU probe) and fetches and plays nothing otherwise (`StoryReel.tsx`, `StoryReel.test.tsx`).
2. "Loading the 3D view" was drawn on top of the Explore button, hiding its label. Over the reel it now sits under the controls (`experience.css`).

README: "A scroll during the opening fast-forwards it" was untrue of the reel (it cuts to the tour); now says so.

The recorded frame has finest-level plants and the live one does not, so a cross-fade shows trees changing detail.
(j) Scene code, not touched: a second Explore logs "THREE.KTX2Loader: Multiple active KTX2 loaders" (the first loader is not disposed on Back), and `THREE.Clock` deprecation warnings.
(k) Chrome and Firefox decode AV1 in software where the device has no AV1 hardware. On a weak phone, seeking the tour may then show fewer frames than H.264 would. Check on the real phone; the order is `REEL_CODECS` in `reel.ts`.
(l) 152 MB of video goes into git once this is committed, and as much again each time the whole reel is re-recorded.
